The project involved the design and construction of a modern indoor hotel pool adapted to the building’s architecture and the way guests use the surrounding relaxation areas.

The main objective was to create a calm and functional environment with a strong visual connection between the pool, natural light, and the surrounding landscape.

The pool was constructed using high-quality materials and a modern water filtration and circulation system. Particular attention was given to the reliability of the technical systems, energy efficiency, and straightforward day-to-day maintenance.
